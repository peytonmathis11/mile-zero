MILE Ø / PERSONAL SETTINGS

Runner profile & heart-rate zones

Optional details help estimate training zones and energy use. You can leave these blank, and edit them at any time. These estimates are not medical assessments.

Weather and location preferences

Optional training context. Use your location on demand or choose a city for local forecasts. Mile Zero can reference recent conditions during training reviews. Your exact GPS coordinates are not saved to your runner profile or sent to Supabase. Coordinates are sent to the weather provider when you request a forecast.

Saved training location (optional)

Save a city or town, not your address. The city name is part of your account profile and syncs to your private Supabase record. Weather is fetched from the provider only when you request it or request a training review. No precise GPS position is saved. You can remove this anytime.
