Runner-approved training intelligence

After you log training, Mile Zero compares planned and actual mileage, how hard sessions felt, recent missed workouts, and fatigue or pain reports. It proposes changes to specific upcoming runs, explains why, and asks you to approve each workout individually. Nothing changes automatically. Declines and approvals are recorded, and approvals can be undone if the sessions have not been modified or logged.

This version uses a transparent adaptive rules engine, not a live AI language model. It does not diagnose injuries or guarantee an appropriate training load. Real model-based AI, wearables, and account syncing require additional infrastructure and may have ongoing costs.
